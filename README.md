## ⚡ Hi, I'm Hassan
 
I'm a **student developer at [Metropolia University of Applied Sciences](https://www.metropolia.fi/en)** in Finland 🇫🇮, building across the whole stack, from the interface a user taps to the database behind it, and from apps on screen to circuits on a desk.
 
Studying gives me strong fundamentals and a hunger to learn. Building on the side gives me the practical experience to back it up. I learn fast, ship real things, and care about the details that make a product feel finished.
